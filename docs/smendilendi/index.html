Smendiłendi

Four servos. One grin.

A small printed robot dog. Hobby servos, an ESP32, a touch sensor on its back, and a head sculpted by hand.

Learn more

A face, not a box

The head is not generated from code. It was sculpted in Blender, eyes, nose, smile and one ear, and it clips onto the body's neck tongue.

Pet it

A capacitive pad under the cap on its back knows when a hand is there. That is the whole input.

It waddles

Four servos, one per leg, no knees. Diagonal pairs swing together and the body rocks. That is the walk, and it stays.

Made in Gzowo. Printed on an X1C. Prototype. Source and STL files on GitHub · Gzowo Labs
